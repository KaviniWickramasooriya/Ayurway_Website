# Ayurway Lanka (Pvt) Ltd
*“Let Nature Protect Your Health and Beauty.”*

A luxury, editorial-style e-commerce platform and digital apothecary for Ayurway, an authentic Sri Lankan Ayurvedic brand established in 2019. Founded by **Dr. Ishara Sandamini** and **Mr. Anura Pathirana**, the brand bridges classical Ayurvedic wisdom with modern cosmetic care.

---

## Brand Ethos & Highlights
* **Clinical Heritage**: Formulated under the guidance of Dr. Ishara Sandamini (Graduate of the Faculty of Indigenous Medicine, University of Colombo).
* **Purity Promise**: 100% natural origin formulations, strictly free from parabens, SLS, and artificial colorants.
* **Atelier Location**: No.58, Yahampath Mawatha, Piliyandala Road, Maharagama, Sri Lanka.

---

## Core Features
* **Cinematic Editorial Design**: Minimalist layout featuring generous whitespace, smooth scroll-reveal animations, custom typography (*Cormorant Garamond* and *Jost*), and a rich earthy palette (Forest Deep, Gold, Sand, and Ivory).
* **Dynamic Apothecary (Shop)**: Category filtering (Body Care, Hair Care, Face Care, Child Care, Herbal Ingredients), live URL-parameter search, and quick-add functionality.
* **Immersive Product Detail Pages (PDP)**: Sticky image galleries, multi-step botanical highlights, and interactive formulation accordions.
* **Interactive Cart & Secure Checkout**: Slide-out cart drawer with real-time subtotal/shipping calculations, floating-label form inputs, and custom payment selection.
* **The Concierge & About Us**: Dedicated brand story pages detailing founder biographies and an interactive, styled Google Map for the Maharagama atelier.

---

## Technical Architecture
* **Frontend Framework**: React
* **Routing**: TanStack Router (with dynamic product routing)
* **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 (with custom design tokens and utility animations)
* **Icons**: Lucide React
* **Build Tool**: Vite
